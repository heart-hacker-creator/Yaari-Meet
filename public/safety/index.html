Safety & community rules

YaariMeet is for friendly conversations between adults. Here's how we keep it that way — and how you can stay safe.

Our rules

  • 18+ only. If you are under 18, you may not use YaariMeet.
  • No nudity or sexual content. Not on camera, not in chat.
  • No harassment, hate or threats. Be kind, even when you press Next.
  • Be who you say you are. Choosing the wrong gender gets you banned.
  • No spam, selling or links to other sites.
  • No recording or screenshots of other people without their consent.

Breaking the rules leads to a permanent ban. Unused credits on banned accounts are not refunded.

Staying safe

  • Never share your full name, address, school, workplace, phone number or social media with strangers.
  • Never send money or share OTPs, passwords or bank details — YaariMeet will never ask for them in a chat.
  • Remember anyone can see whatever your camera shows. Check your background.
  • If anyone makes you uncomfortable, press Next straight away, then Report.

How reporting works

Tap Report during a chat and choose Abusive or Wrong gender. The report goes to our moderation team with the account details, and we ban accounts that break the rules. In an emergency, contact your local police or call 112. For cyber crime in India, report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
