Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

What we collect

  • Account details from Google: your name and email address.
  • Profile details you give us: display name and gender.
  • Payment details: the amount and UPI transaction ID (UTR) you submit. We never see your bank or card details.
  • Verification videos: a 3-second selfie video, only if you choose to verify. It is deleted as soon as our team reviews it.
  • Safety data: reports you make or receive, and your IP address, used to fight abuse.

What we don't collect

Video and audio calls travel directly between you and the other person (peer-to-peer, encrypted with WebRTC). We do not record or store your calls. Text messages are passed through our server to the other person and are not stored.

How we use your data

To run your account, match you with other people, add credits you pay for, verify profiles, enforce our rules, and keep the service secure.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use trusted providers to run the service: Google (sign-in), Supabase (database) and Cloudflare (hosting). We may share data with authorities when the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing contact@yaarimeet.com. Deleting your account removes your profile and remaining credits.

Children

YaariMeet is only for people aged 18 and over. We delete accounts that belong to minors.
